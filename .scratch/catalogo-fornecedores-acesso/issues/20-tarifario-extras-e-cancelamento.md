# 20: Tarifário extras, group rates, cancellation and "a confirmar"

**What to build:** The rest of what a hotel charges and the terms it sets:
- **Extras:** breakfast in advance or on site (sometimes with tax included, Paradise 42,350), extra person, extra bed.
- **Guest rules:** child ages (in months where the source uses them: 36, 48, 49), "no children under 12" (Nostalgia Blue and Double), one adult per room (K672), no third person or extra bed at one unit (Shinshin Jeju Airport).
- **Group or FIT:** a surcharge per room below the group size (Stanford +11,000 below 8 rooms, Homm +22,000), separate group and FIT grids (Park Sunshine), group thresholds per hotel.
- **Cancellation:** tiers that change by season (Hidden Cliff free to 8 days, 11 in high season) and by group (Paradise 30+ rooms), deadlines with a time of day ("2 days before, 18h"), the penalty basis (one night, first night, whole stay, each night, rooms plus food), changes charged as cancellations, the supplier's office hours (requests after 9–18h on working days count from the next day), block reductions, refunds as milestones.
- **"A confirmar":** a value the source leaves ambiguous is entered "a confirmar" and never used silently.
