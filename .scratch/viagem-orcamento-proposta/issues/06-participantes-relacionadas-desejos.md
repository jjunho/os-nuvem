# 06: Participants, Viagens relacionadas, wants and approvals

**What to build:** A salesperson adds other staff as participants on a Viagem, so several people can follow one case. They link Viagens relacionadas when a group is split into separately priced parts. They keep a list of what the Cliente wants and what the Cliente approved on the Viagem, so negotiation doesn't depend on chat history. Spec: stories 27, 79, 89.

**Blocked by:** 01 (Known options: a filtered list with an open last option).

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Adding a participant shows them on the Viagem. Removing them keeps the history.
- [ ] Linking two Viagens shows each on the other. Unlinking works from either side.
- [ ] A wanted item can be added, marked approved (with who and when) or dropped. The list shows both states.
