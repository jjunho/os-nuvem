# 15: Roteiro operacional and the Guia's view

**What to build:** The Roteiro operacional for the Equipe: times, addresses, bookings, Pendências, notes and Observações para a Equipe, separate from the client document. It prints in Portuguese or Korean and exports as text for outside Profissionais, who don't sign in. A Guia signed in sees their own Alocações and the Roteiro operacional of those Dias, with Incluso and Não incluso and no value (Catálogo 16). Spec: `.scratch/operacao-e-pagamentos/spec.md` (stories 21, 26, 35 of the Catálogo spec).
