# 13: Voucher v1

**What to build:** The Voucher, in the Idioma do cliente, generated from the operational plan and never retyped. It has its own number `AAAAMMDD-N` from the arrival date (K305), with the Código da viagem also printed. **The number is recorded for audit:** it is generated by the system, stored with the Voucher, unique and never reused (not even after a cancelamento), and kept across versions; the arrival date that produced it is stored with it, so a later date change never renumbers it. Every issue records who, when and which version. It holds:
- the Responsável and the **team phone** always; each Dia's Guia and phone as an option, on by default only when that Dia's Alocação is confirmed (K249, K304);
- the Viajantes and the emergency contact;
- Receptivos and deslocamentos with the person in charge;
- only the Hospedagem we booked, Itens de terceiros only when chosen, with check-in and check-out and the notes that bookings are in the client's name (check-in with passport only) and hotel extras are paid at check-out (K846, K633);
- Guiamento per Dia with meeting time and activities, generic names ("bondinho") and "horário a confirmar" for items whose window hasn't opened, "almoço não incluído" on guided days;
- each person's KTX car and seat, and "as passagens de trem serão enviadas 1 mês antes" (K306);
- the Equipe introduced with name, photo and role (Catálogo 15), and every booking code;
- the footer: tours may change with weather and traffic (K307), and "CoreaLux is a trade name of Coreanissima Co., Ltd" with the tourism registration (K755, ticket 12).
